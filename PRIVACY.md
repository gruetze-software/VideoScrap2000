## Your media library

VideoScrap 2000 reads files from the local folders or network shares you select. It uses file names and video files to identify titles and read technical media information. Settings and the library cache are stored on your device. When you save changes, the app writes metadata and artwork alongside your media files. The app does not upload your video files or your library database.

## Online metadata and artwork

When you use online lookup features, VideoScrap 2000 contacts services such as TMDB, Fanart.tv, TVmaze, and TheTVDB to retrieve metadata and artwork. Depending on the feature, requests may include a title, release year, media identifier, or language preference. These services receive your IP address as part of the connection and process requests under their own privacy policies.

## External websites and optional AI prompts

VideoScrap 2000 can prepare a prompt for use with an external AI service. The app does not submit the prompt for you. If you paste it into an external service, that service processes it under its own privacy policy. External links open in your browser.

## Data controls

You can remove folders from the app and clear its locally stored settings and cache. Metadata and artwork saved alongside your media remain in those folders until you remove them. If you contact us about privacy, we use the information you provide to respond to your request.
